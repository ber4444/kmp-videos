# Historical evaluation tables

These aggregate tables preserve the numerical sources cited by the Medium drafts.
The scorecard was recomputed offline from the existing v5 fixture archive; context
and calibration tables were retained from their recorded reports. No new provider
calls were made and no audio, hypotheses or reference transcripts are included.

Treat prose labels such as "full context", "floor", "perfect" and "ceiling" as
historical experimental assumptions. The recovered translation fixtures do not
preserve enough request configuration to establish matched context, and agreement
between two machine translations is not a human quality ceiling. The 2026 refresh
report qualifies those limits explicitly. Calibration's initial Hungarian in-band
rows cover five clips; its controls and two-stage rows cover 21.

The scorecard's transcript alignment links were omitted because those files contain
unpublished reference and hypothesis text. All aggregate table values are retained.

`metric_calibration.paired.{hu,ru}.md` are later offline rescores of the full
21-clip archive. They retain clip identity in every cell and compute every lead
and gap on the intersection of the two arms. The original calibration reports
remain unchanged; their in-band rows covered five clips.
