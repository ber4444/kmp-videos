Privacy Policy

Apollo Videos · Effective 28 August 2026

Apollo Videos does not collect your data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer operates exactly one small service, and it exists to protect the caption provider’s credentials rather than to collect anything about you: when you switch captions on, the app asks it for a key that expires in a minute. It is sent no audio. It does check your existing Discord sign-in, so that only Apollo members can spend the transcription account, and it stores nothing.

The app is gated by the Apollo Discord server, and its sole purpose is playing media files.

Apollo Videos is developed by Gabor Berenyi. Its complete source code is public at github.com/ber4444/kmp-videos, so every statement on this page can be checked against the code that makes it true, rather than taken on trust.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDK is present in the app.
  • No user profile, account, or record is created or stored anywhere by the developer.
  • The developer’s only server issues short-lived caption keys. It never receives your audio or anything you do in the app. It checks your Discord sign-in to confirm Apollo membership, exactly as the app already does, and keeps no records.
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, files, or location. The app does not request those permissions, so it cannot use them.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with data brokers, advertisers, or anyone else.

What leaves your device

The app is a media player, so it makes network requests to play video and to check that you are allowed in. Those requests go directly from your device to the services below — never through the developer. Each one necessarily sees your IP address and the timing of your requests, as is the case for any website you visit.

About the Discord sign-in

Access to the feed is limited to members of the Apollo Discord server, so the app has to ask Discord who you are. It uses the standard authorization-code flow with PKCE, and requests the two narrowest scopes that answer the question: identify and guilds.

  • Your server list is examined in memory to answer one yes-or-no question — are you on Apollo? — and is then discarded. It is never written to storage and never leaves your device.
  • Your display name is kept on your device only, so the app can greet you without asking Discord again on every screen.
  • Your Discord password is never seen by the app. You type it on Discord's own pages, and the app never receives it.
  • Membership is re-checked at every launch, so leaving the Apollo server revokes your access at the next start.

What stays on your device

The app stores a few things locally, in its own private storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • A Discord refresh token, so you sign in once rather than at every launch. It is held in the most protected per-app store each platform offers: SharedPreferences on Android, the Keychain on iOS, localStorage on the web.
  • Your Discord display name, purely to show it while restoring your session.
  • Thumbnail images extracted from videos, cached so the feed does not re-download them.
  • Videos you chose to download for offline viewing.
  • The cached list of extra videos described above.

Signing out erases the stored session. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in system settings, erases everything above. On the web build, clearing site data does the same.

Permissions

The Android app requests only what the media player needs to work:

  • Internet — to stream video.
  • Foreground service (media playback and data sync) — to keep audio playing and downloads progressing when the app is not on screen.
  • Notifications — for playback controls on the lock screen and download progress. Declining costs you those notifications, nothing else.
  • Wake lock — to let the media notification appear above the lock screen.

Google Play Data Safety

In the Google Play Data Safety form, this app declares No data collected and No data shared with third parties.

That declaration reflects the behaviour described above: the app transmits nothing about you to the developer, keeps no record of you anywhere, and stores what little it holds on your own device. The requests it makes to Discord, to the streaming server, and to the caption provider are actions you initiate, sent directly from your device to services you have chosen to use.

Children

Apollo Videos is not directed at children, and is not designed or marketed for them. It knowingly collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
