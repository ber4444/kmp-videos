Privacy Policy

Apollo Videos · Effective 13 November 2026

Apollo Videos plays live and recorded events for the Apollo community. It uses your existing Discord account to verify access and offers optional live captions through Soniox. The app has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Apollo Videos is developed by Gabor Berenyi. View the source code.

Retention and storage

The feed and caption service uses Discord profile and membership information to decide whether to grant access. It does not persist ordinary users’ Discord IDs, guild lists, or raw access tokens. It stores a token-derived lookup key and the access decision in memory for reuse for up to five minutes. Expired entries can remain in memory until removed or the service restarts. The service keeps IP-based rate limiting counters in memory and produces operational logs that include request routes and response status.

The app saves the Discord refresh token and display name in Android app preferences, the iOS Keychain, or web browser storage. Depending on your device settings, the operating system may include Android preferences or eligible iOS Keychain entries in backup or device migration. Deleting the app can leave its iOS Keychain entry behind. The iOS app clears the saved session when it detects a fresh install. The app also caches thumbnails and saves recordings you choose to download.

Your choices

You can stop sending playback audio to Soniox by switching off live captions. Use Sign out where available to clear the app’s saved Discord session. To withdraw the authorization itself, remove Apollo Videos from Authorized Apps in Discord settings. You can remove downloaded videos from the app and clear local app or site data using your device or browser settings. Apollo Videos does not create a separate account.

For a privacy or deletion request, use the contact link below. Please keep access tokens, passwords, and other private information out of public issues.

Permissions

The Android app requests only what the media player needs to work:

  • Internet — to stream video.
  • Foreground service (media playback and data sync) — to keep audio playing and downloads progressing when the app is not on screen.
  • Notifications — for playback controls on the lock screen and download progress. Declining costs you those notifications, nothing else.
  • Wake lock — to let the media notification appear above the lock screen.

Children

Apollo Videos is intended for teens and adults who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements. It is not directed to children under 13.
